About us

GemTale sits at the intersection of emotional consumer tech and playful accessories. Mimir is a friendly spirit figure (3D-printed, bright colors) carrying a gem — the physical token for a cloud adventure log. Hold your phone against him once when you receive him; then log moods, notes, and photos anytime. Forward Mimir to a friend: your chapter freezes, theirs begins, and you can follow the traveling gem's story as it moves from person to person.

Where we stand today: a working web app with daily entries, story generation, multi-gem timelines and handover with chapter freeze; live NFC tap-to-pair on the first physical spirits; and a printable v4 enclosure. Founded 2025 in Grafing near Munich, founder-led.

Vision

We believe objects should carry human experience — not precious metal, but stories that travel. A spirit passed from friend to friend, each adding a chapter with photos and AI-crafted entries. The gem it carries is the thread. Our vision: the first companion whose story you can forward and follow — one chapter per owner, preserved forever.

On specs we lose to any gadget; on feeling we have a character with a myth and a mechanic. Giving an object away is counter-intuitive — in Mimir's story it is inevitable. He was always going to fall onward. He fell into a human's life once. Now it's yours.

    About 18 g of recyclable PETG, held by three M2 screws so a half or the tag can be replaced. No firmware to go unsupported. The one evening ritual: mood, a few words, a photo — one entry per gem per day, publishable to several spirits at once.

Launch: one sculpt, multiple bright body colorways and gem colors at checkout. €12.99 in a printed sleeve, or €19.99 hand-painted in a gift box — no subscription either way. 36 × 40 × 41 mm on a bag strap or keyring. Printed in PETG. Inside: a single passive NFC tag — no battery, no board, no charging port.

A passive tag is not a radio transmitter: no radio certification, no battery regulation, no lithium shipping rules. The story engine runs rule-based and offline — about 280 phrases across ten themes and four moods — so an entry costs nothing to generate; a language model is an optional upgrade, not a dependency.

For investors

The first traveling story companion

GemTale connects a daily adventure log (AI + photos) to Mimir, a 3D-printed spirit carrying a gem — designed to be forwarded between people. No subscription: the spirit from €12.99, a free app, a one-time €24.99 unlock, and printed keepsakes of the story. Adults 25–55, gift & friend-forward occasions. Founded 2025, HQ Munich. Seeking €650k Seed.

Market

Emotional consumer tech · keepsakes · digital wellness · meaningful gifts. SAM DACH + EU: €500M–€1B over 5 years. Beachhead: DACH gift occasions and family/mindfulness buyers; people want digital content anchored in something physical.

Model

No subscription. Spirits from €12.99, one-time €24.99 unlock, printed Year Books, B2B editions. Illustrative Year 3 revenue ~€1.8M, gross margin ~70%. Keepsakes are print-on-demand: the Year Book each January, a Chapter Book at handover, the Full Tale once a spirit has had three owners.

Moat

A named character with a story behind him + AI adventure log + forward/follow mechanic. Competitors can copy a printed figure with a chip in a quarter; they cannot copy a character people have already handed to someone they love.

Unit economics

~€3.26 to make, sold from €12.99 — a ~70% margin. Priced to be an easy yes, not to invent volume: Year 3 is tens of thousands of spirits, not a hundred thousand. Acquisition cost is ~€0 via handover, gifting and B2B — the partner's brand does the storytelling.

Because the shell is 3D-printed, a co-branded edition costs nothing in tooling — a partner colorway with embossed logo is a file edit and a filament change, at roughly 500-unit minimums. Outdoor and travel brands, corporate gifting and house gems that stay at a hotel or mountain hut are the volume engine from Year 2 — thousands of activated users, funded by the partner's marketing.
