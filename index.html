About us

GemTale sits at the intersection of emotional consumer tech and fine jewelry. We build a physical gem paired with a mobile app that asks short questions every evening — mood, experiences, gratitude — and uses AI to shape them into personal diary entries. Users can add their own thoughts and images. After syncing with the gem via Bluetooth, the entry becomes part of a living timeline tied to that object. When the gem is passed on, the current chapter freezes. Both the previous and new owner can read every frozen chapter and follow the active one as it grows. The value is not technology. The value is memory — carried across people and generations.

Vision

We believe jewelry should carry more than precious materials — it should carry human experience. GemTale creates emotional objects that grow more valuable over time, not because of gold or silver, but because of the stories they contain. A gem passed from mother to daughter. A bracelet shared between lifelong friends. A necklace that travels from owner to owner, each person leaving one frozen chapter before the next begins. Our vision is the first heirloom that grows with AI and is passed from person to person — one chapter at a time, preserved forever.
